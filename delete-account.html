Delete your GK Master account

GK Master · Last updated 13 November 2026

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open GK Master and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all progress saved to it are deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it (without the app)

Email shunyarachna@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my GK Master account". We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and account ID).
  • All progress saved to your account: answers, scores, quiz sessions, bookmarks, mistakes and settings.
  • Any Pro access granted to your account by a redeemed code.

What is not deleted by us

  • Google Play subscription records, which Google keeps under its own policy. Cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store app before deleting your account.
  • Data stored only on your phone: uninstall the app to remove it.
  • Anonymous records with no link to you (for example cached answers to typed questions), and records we must keep by law.

See also our privacy policy.
